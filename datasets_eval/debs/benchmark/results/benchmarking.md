| `per_pair_rel_err_min` | 5.149e-07 | — | ✓ |
| `per_pair_rel_err_max` | 0.2528 | ≤ 0.0100 | ✗ |

> **Q1 note (60-min run, 09:00 start):** `frac_lt_1pct` is 0.9050, below the 0.95 threshold. The market-open window (09:00–09:05 CET) has significantly higher price volatility than the pre-market period, causing EMA38 sketch estimates to diverge for a small fraction of (symbol, window) pairs. The mean relative error remains low (0.0061) but a handful of outliers push the max to 0.2528. Running with `--accuracy-minutes 300` at `--speed 5` would cover 59 non-warmup windows and give a more representative picture across the full trading morning.
